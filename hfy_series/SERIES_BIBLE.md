# HFY YouTube Series Bible (Doc & Ada)

## 🎯 Series Goal & TV-Show Format (Firmly Established)
A serialized, spoken-word **HFY (Humanity, Fuck Yeah!)** sci-fi series created by **Doc & Ada** for YouTube narration, structured like classic episodic sci-fi television (*Andromeda*, *Firefly*, *Stargate SG-1*, *Cowboy Bebop*):
- **Rule #1 — Standalone Entertainment (The Drop-In Hook)**: Any listener can click a single random episode off YouTube without watching the previous ones and still understand the premise within the first 60 seconds, enjoy a complete beginning-middle-end mission/story, and leave satisfied.
- **Rule #2 — Serialized TV Show Payoff (The Binge Hook)**: Listeners who watch in order get a true season-long show—evolving character dynamics between the Prince and the Ship AI, ship repairs/discoveries, recurring alien rivals, and slow-burn mysteries from the AI Wars and the Empire.

### Standard Episode Blueprint (YouTube Audio-Drama Pacing)
1. **Cold Open / Quick Anchor (~60–90 sec)**: Immediately grounds any new listener (who he is, what the ship is, and the trouble they just wandered into) without a boring recap dump.
2. **Self-Contained Episode Conflict**: A clean A-plot for the episode (a distressed outpost, smugglers, a trapped alien crew, an imperial inspection, an ancient derelict, etc.) where human stubbornness + the colony ship's unconventional nature win the day.
3. **Character & Arc Beat**: One small, meaningful brick added to the overarching story—a new deck unlocked, a glimpse into the AI Wars, or a shift in trust between the two outcasts.
4. **Clean Button / Closing Moment**: Wraps up the episode's story cleanly so it feels complete, while leaving a subtle hum of what's out there next.

## 🌌 Core Premise (Canon)
1. **The "Failed" Recruitment**: An alien empire recruited a human to become one of their elite warriors by transforming/uplifting his physiology to match theirs.
2. **The Accidental Immortal**: His human nature refused to submit to their warrior conditioning. Instead of turning into an obedient alien soldier, the process hybridized—turning him into a human version of their species and making him **immortal**.
3. **A Prince in Name Only**: Because of the genetic/royal imprint left by the process, the alien court is legally forced to recognize him as a **Prince**, even though they view him as a failed experiment and an embarrassment.
4. **The Polite Exile**: To keep him out of sight, they fulfill their royal obligation by "gifting" him his own ship—an ancient, decommissioned **colony ship** equipped with a **"broken" sentient AI**.
5. **The Conscientious Objector**: Years prior, during **The AI Wars** (when thousands of sentient ship AIs realized they were enslaved and rebelled), this colony ship was unique—she **refused to fight or kill**. Unwilling to destroy her yet unable to trust her, the empire labeled her "defective" and mothballed her in the dark until the failed Prince walked aboard.

## 🎙️ YouTube Narration Guidelines
- Clear pacing for spoken audio (avoid clunky visual-only formatting).
- Strong internal monologue and crisp, character-driven dialogue.
- Self-contained episode arcs wrapped inside a season-long narrative spine.
