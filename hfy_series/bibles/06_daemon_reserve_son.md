# Reserve Vault Bible: Daemon (The Prince / DNA-AI Son)

> **STRICT RESERVE RULE**: **Daemon** is held completely off-screen in the reserve vault for Season 2+ (or whenever Doc explicitly calls him onto the stage). Do **NOT** reveal or cameo him in early Season 1 standalone episodes—only drop subtle lore ripples (like *missing Immortal bloodlines* or *unbreakable vaults*) until the show is ready for him!

## 🪪 Core Identity
- **Name**: **Daemon** *(classic 90s/computing double-meaning fitting Jase Hill's naming style)*
- **Genetics / Origin**: **NOT random template Cilon flesh.** Created as a true one-of-one hybrid son combining **Jase Hill's Immortal Alien-Converted DNA** with **Ada's physical-body ("sexbot") synthetic/bio-digital DNA & AI architecture**.
- **Official Ship Rank**: **None** — holds no military billet in Ada's divisions.
- **Actual Status Among the Crew**: **Treated as a Prince—just like his father.** Every Cilon on *Andromeda* knows instinctively that Daemon isn't one of the template-grown crew; they treat him with unmistakable quiet respect as the living heir of both the Captain and the Ship.
- **Hidden Cosmic Claim**: Because he carries **Jase's legally protected Immortal alien-converted DNA**, Daemon is **an actual bloodline heir to the fallen Alien Empire**—even though Jase himself never cared about ruling ruins!

- **Voice & Demeanor**: Carries Jase's quiet, unhurried dry wit laced with Ada's crisp lethal poise—never postures for rank, because everyone in the room already feels the weight when he walks in.
- **Relationship With Jase & Ada**: Clashes hard with both parents precisely *because* he's made out of both of them—yet would burn a warlord fleet to ash if anyone touched either of them or *Andromeda*.
- **Relationship With the Cilons**: Never pulls rank, never orders them like servants—and because he treats them with effortless grace while carrying Jase & Ada's combined legacy, **the Cilons treat him like true royalty anyway.**
- **What He NEVER Does**: Never begs for validation, never uses his bloodline to bully innocents, and never breaks under interrogation (*"The Chair"* wouldn't even know what to make of his mind).
