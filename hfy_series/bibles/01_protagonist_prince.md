## 🧬 Origin & "Failure"

- **The Transformation**: The empire attempted to genetically rewrite the recruits into elite mercenary warriors. Many flunked out as unfit to be changed; many others **did not survive the brutal conversion**. Jase survived, but his human biology and settled early-40s mind refused to be wiped clean—making him an **immortal human version of them** while keeping his human soul, stubbornness, and free will intact.
