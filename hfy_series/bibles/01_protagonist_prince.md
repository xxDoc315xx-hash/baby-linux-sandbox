# Character Bible: Jase Hill (The Immortal Prince)

## 🪪 Core Identity
- **Name**: **Jase Hill**
- **Age at Recruitment**: Early 40s (now functionally **immortal**)
- **Title**: Prince (In name only — legally protected status because **Immortals are a protected race**)
- **Species**: Uplifted / Hybridized Human (Immortal human version of the alien species)
- **Role**: Outcast "Failed Warrior," Captain of the Mothballed Colony Ship

## 🧬 Origin & "Failure"
- **How He Was Recruited**: Earth traded **100,000 humans** to the Peacekeeper-style mercenary empire in exchange for **advanced alien technology**. Jase, in his early 40s with nothing left tying him to Earth, **volunteered** for the transfer.
- **The Chair (Evaluation & Sorting)**: Every recruit is strapped into **"The Chair"** (inspired by *Farscape*'s Aurora Chair)—a neural/aptitude machine that probes their mind and biology to judge if they are worthy and reveal **what role they would be good for**. Some flunked out immediately as unfit; others didn't survive the conversion.
- **The Transformation**: After the Aliens worked on everyone, Jase didn't turn into an obedient grunt—the conversion made him an **immortal human version of them**.
- **The Legal Loophole (Why He's a "Prince")**: In the galaxy, **Immortals are a legally protected race**. The Aliens couldn't force Jase into the meat grinder as a mercenary soldier, and they couldn't execute him to cover up their "failed" experiment. Bound by law to recognize his protected noble status, they slapped an empty **Prince** title on him and kicked him out of sight with an old colony ship and a "broken" AI.
