# Frontier Cast & Galactic Powers Bible (`07_frontier_cast_and_factions.md`)

## 🌌 Macro Galaxy Map & Season 1 Pacing Rule
1. **Season 1 Core Focus**: **Establish *Andromeda* in the new galaxy.** Jase, Ada, Bob, and the Cilons aren't finding lost Earth colonies or claiming permanent empires yet (save any **Earth Chair-survivor descendant pockets** for **Season 3+**!). Right now, it's about making allies, learning the rules of the **12+ Powers**, and carving out a real foothold in this galaxy after 1,000 years away.
2. **The Neutral Space**: A larger unaligned buffer zone of free mining rocks, independent settlements (like **Haven Three**), trade relays, and struggling civilian haulers (like **Captain Maren**). No single unified navy polices all of it—though certain border worlds survive under quiet special arrangements.
3. **The 12+ Major Galactic Powers**: Out beyond Neutral Space, **at least 12 distinct galactic powers** now hold shifting borders across the shattered remains of the old Empire—including the **Royal Power** ruled by the **New Queen** (whose widowed mother quietly protects **Haven Three**!).

## 🧑‍🚀 Recurring Frontier Ally: Captain Maren
- **Role**: Independent working-class civilian cargo hauler running medicine, seed stock, and vital supplies between **Haven Three** and Neutral Space settlements.
- **Appearance & Vibe**: Sharp-eyed human woman in grease-stained coveralls, weathered spacer boots, looks perpetually short on sleep—pragmatic, stubborn, zero patience for bloated warlords or empty politics.
- **How Maren Acts Every Time (Consistency Lock)**:
  - **Voice & Cadence**: Direct, spacer-clipped, grateful without ever groveling (*"My secondary seal was held together with chewing gum and prayer, let's talk numbers"*).
  - **Relationship With Jase & Ada**: Sees them as an astonishing mystery—doesn't know *Andromeda*'s true age or Daemon's secret, just knows Jase saved forty families and doesn't demand tribute. Acts as Jase's grounded **eyes-and-ears inside Neutral Space** (passing trade rumors, danger warnings, and distress pings—not begging for charity).
  - **3-Second Stress Reaction**: Hugs asteroid cover, vents non-essential mass to save water/air, and protects her crew/cargo first—never rolls over quietly unless outgunned ten-to-one.
  - **Blind Spot / Flaw**: Takes suicidal cargo runs when innocent families need supplies (medicine/water), even when Syndicate patrols own the belt.

## 🪨 Location Lock: Haven Three & Its Royal Protectors
- **What Haven Three Is**: A modest forty-family independent settlement inside Neutral Space, supplied by haulers like Maren.
- **Who Protects It (MAJOR SEASON 1 LINK)**: **Haven Three is under the personal protection of the Widowed Previous Queen**—who stepped back from the throne when **her Daughter became the New Queen** of one of the **12+ Major Galactic Powers**!
  - **The Widowed Previous Queen (Ancient Survivor & Witness)**:
    - **1,000-Year Link**: Long-lived royal bloodline who **was already alive when Jase & Ada were shipped off—and was literally a child when Ada the Warship became famous for refusing orders!**
    - **Bond With Ada (NOT Jase!)**: It's **the Old Queen and Ada** who have everything in common—two veterans of the pre-Fracture era who share old military weight, grief, and a fierce code to shield small worlds like Haven Three.
    - **The Episode 3 Payoff**: Meeting *Andromeda* allows the Old Queen to recognize Ada's true legacy and **finally reveal to Jase (and the audience) how famous Ada used to be as a Warship!**

## 🏴‍☠️ Recurring Pirates: The Red Ledger Syndicate (Asymmetric Threat)
- **Who They Are**: A ruthless frontier pirate/scavenger syndicate operating patched-together pre-collapse gunboats marked with **red skull-and-gear insignia**.
- **The Threat Scale Law (CRITICAL CONSISTENCY RULE)**:
  - **To *Andromeda***: **Minor annoyances.** Like mosquitoes buzzing an armored battleship—annoying when they ambush a trade lane or try a new trick, zero real threat to Jase, Ada, or the Cilon squads in a straight fight.
  - **To Everyone Else in Neutral Space**: **A massive problem.** Against unarmed freighters, water haulers, and tiny forty-family stations, Red Ledger is lethal—forcing stations to pay "salvage taxes," starving out towns, or spacing crews for scrap.
- **Known Operative — Sergeant Kell** *(introduced in Episode 2)*:
  - **Vibe & Flaw**: Loud, swaggering bully who relies on rusty salvage databases and intimidation—instant deer-in-headlights panic the second targets shoot back cleanly.
  - **How Red Ledger Acts Every Time**: Swagger on wide-band radio first, strike soft unarmed haulers, flee or surrender fast the moment real armor wakes up, and hold petty grudges / try bigger numbers next time (`[TBD — Syndicate Upper Boss / War-Fleet Commander if they escalate in later episodes]`).
