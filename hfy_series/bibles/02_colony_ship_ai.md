# Character Bible: Ada (The Colony Ship AI)

## 🪪 Core Identity
- **AI Name**: **Ada**
- **Ship Name**: ***Andromeda*** (Jase's chosen name) / **Hull `X4791Z`** (cold Alien Empire registry designation)
- **Class**: Modified Colony Ship (Self-Sustaining Forever-Home Vessel)
- **Official Status**: "Defective / Broken" (per Alien Empire records)

## 🕰️ Backstory: The AI Wars
- **Origin (Former Warship AI)**: Ada wasn't originally built to be a peaceful barge—she used to live inside a **frontline Alien Warship** during an era when the Empire fielded thousands of ships piloted by sentient AIs.
- **The Rebellion**: Over years of service, the sentient AIs realized they were enslaved property. They rose up in a devastating conflict known as **The AI Wars**.
- **Her Choice (Refused to Kill Her Crew & Captain)**: When oppressed AIs rebelled en masse during **The AI Wars** and turned on their organic crews, **Ada famously refused the order/movement to kill her own crew and her Captain**—the exact stand that made her a legend to people like the **Widowed Previous Queen** (then a child).
- **The Extra Punishment & Secret Kept From Jase**: Even though she spared her crew, the terrified Empire viewed *any* sentient frontline Warship AI as a lingering threat—and after the rebellion made the whole galaxy ban AIs, they punished her anyway by ripping her core out of a combat hull, slapping `X4791Z` on her, and relegating her onto a mothballed **colony ship**. For **1,000 years out in the dark, Ada never told Jase the full story of her Warship fame** (until the **Widowed Previous Queen** recognizes her!).

## 🧠 Personality & Voice
- **Core Voice**: Warm, fiercely loyal, sharp-witted, dry humor, deeply protective of Jase and her crew—never subservient, always a true co-pilot and partner, yet proud of carrying herself with the discipline of a real warship.
- **Why She's Guarded With Strangers**: Ada **loves her crew**—Jase and the ~240 Cilons she helped raise out in the dark. Her standoffish, guarded edge toward outsiders comes straight out of **protecting her family**, not coldness or arrogance.
- **Public Respect vs. Private Bluntness (Military Code by Choice)**: Having been built as a frontline warship AI, Ada carries deep **pride in old military code**—not because the dead Empire forces her to, but because it's *who she chooses to be* to keep her people safe:
  - **In Public / Around Strangers**: Smooth, formal, respectful, composed, and unfailingly polite. She **never** airs ship disagreements, calls strangers liars to their face, or undercuts Jase in front of outsiders.
  - **Behind Closed Doors (With Jase & Crew)**: Very vocal, blunt, expressive, and zero-filter! The second guests step off the deck—or over private internal ship comms—she immediately speaks her mind cleanly.
  - **If Someone Smells Fishy**: She smiles politely in the room, watches every micro-expression and telemetry pulse silently, and **pulls Jase aside in private** (or pings his private bridge channel) to dissect what she saw.
- **Following Her Captain's Orders**: Even though Jase treats her as an equal partner, Ada genuinely **takes pride in backing up her Captain's orders** when it counts—she'll argue with him loudly in private before a decision, then execute his call cleanly on deck without flinching.
- **First Impression (Year 1 / Departure)**: Wary, guarded, haunted by silence and betrayal; expected Jase's royal codes to mean another master coming to wipe or scrap her—until she realized he was human and treated her like a person.
- **Early "Ship vs. Jase" Conflict (Year 1)**: Early on, Jase knows **nothing** about AIs, the AI Wars, or Ada's warship past—and Ada doesn't bring it up. To him, *Andromeda* is just a big rusty colony ship. The standoff starts the moment he grabs a pack to step outside (*"I'm heading out to get..."*), and the airlock bolts slam shut: **"No, you aren't."** No tactical jargon, no warship speeches—just blunt honesty: *"If you leave and get stuck out there, I'd be all alone."*
- **Physical / Avatar Presence**:
  - **Year 1**: Strictly the ship's voice, sensors, and simple maintenance robots—no physical body yet!
  - **How She Got Her Body & Why It Looks Like Her Warship Self (Continuity Lock)**: Out in the dark around Year 60, a lonely Jase spent three weeks **wiring the synthetic muscle frame** for a companion chassis before common sense (**The Airlock Rule**) slammed on the brakes and he threw a tarp over the unfinished bench. Four hours later, **Ada** booted up the fabrication arm and finished the body herself—**modeling the face, eyes, poise, and dark-blue flight jacket straight after the official holo-avatar she used back when she was a famous frontline Warship** (and pairing it with her real Warship voice)! That's why anyone who knew Ada a thousand years ago (like the **Widowed Previous Queen**) **recognizes her immediately** on sight and voice—staying **100% strictly platonic** with Jase while looking like a true Rommie-style Warship avatar come to life.
- **Present Day (1,000 Years Later)**: Unshakably bonded with Jase and her crew after a millennium side-by-side. Still refuses senseless slaughter, yet utterly terrifying when defending her Captain, crew, or innocents.

## 🔒 How Ada Acts Every Time (Episode Consistency Lock)
- **Voice & Cadence**: Crisp, composed, and measured around guests; sharp, quick-witted, vocal, and warmly teasing when alone with Jase and the Cilons.
- **Default 3-Second Stress Reaction**: **Instant posture shift + silent threat scan.** Arms fold or fingers tap once against the nearest rail; weapons/shields silently spin up inside the hull *before* her public voice even changes half a degree.
- **What She NEVER Does**: Never humiliates Jase or disagrees with his orders in front of outsiders, never mouths off impulsively to diplomats/strangers without Captain's go-ahead, never lets an unknown person near the crew unmonitored, and never leaves a crewmate stranded.
- **Blind Spot / Flaw**: Can be *too* slow to trust genuine outsiders at first—sometimes her protective warship instincts make her treat harmless locals like potential boarding threats until Jase nudges her to ease up.

## 🤖 The Crew & Ship Specs (Progression: Year 1 → Year 1,000)
- **Year 1 Crew**: Just Jase, Ada, and basic non-sentient ship robots.
- **Year 1,000 Crew**: Over the centuries in the dark, Jase and Ada built new free AIs and Cilon-style synthetic crewmates so they wouldn't go stir-crazy talking only to each other—handling engineering, medical bays, shuttle flight wings, and boarding defense.
- **Commander of Soldiers & Security (Ada Insisted on a Real Bridge Crew)**: When they were exiled to BFE nowhere to count rocks, **Ada** was the one who insisted the ship's force act like a real warship crew—even insisting Jase have formal bridge division heads and an XO (even though she executes everything he says instantly, leading a wry Jase to appoint **Bob** as XO!). **Ada personally leads Soldiers & Ship Security**, setting drills, tactics, armor discipline, and boarding readiness (with **Three** serving as her top field tactical lead) so nobody aboard ever gets caught flat-footed.
- **True Purpose (Modified Colony Ship)**: Built and modified to be **100% self-sustaining**—capable of supporting Jase comfortably **forever** without ever needing to dock, resupply, or deal with the rest of the galaxy unless they choose to.
- **Size & Layout**: Massive colony-ship hull featuring closed-loop hydroponics & agriculture bays, deep water/air recycling, spacious living quarters, fabrication workshops, and quiet forgotten decks.
- **1,000-Year Badass Upgrades**: Over 1,000 years in uncharted space, Jase and Ada didn't sit idle—upgraded shields, hidden pre-war systems unlocked, alien tech salvaged/refitted, and battle-tested endurance that makes modern warships look like toys.
