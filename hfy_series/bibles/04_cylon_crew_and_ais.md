# Crew & AI Bible: The Synthetic Crew & Created AIs

## 💡 Why They Exist (1,000 Years in the Dark)
- **Psychological Realism ("Only So Many Games of Chess")**: Even with an unbreakable bond, **1,000 years alone in deep space with only two voices** would drive anyone stir-crazy—there are only so many times Jase and Ada can play chess against each other!
- **The First Spark & The Core Question**: It doesn't start as some grand tactical plan—it starts casually with Jase floating the idea of making a **chess partner**. And right away, Jase asks the deep, grounded question at the heart of all their creations: ***"If we make one... is this just a different version of you, or actually someone new?"***
- **Raised & Parented, Not Programmed ("So They Wouldn't Be Robots")**: That question gives Jase and Ada their answer—you don't copy Ada's mind or code an obedient tool. **They raise the new AIs. They parent them.** Instead of compiling pre-packaged adult software, they nurture new minds from scratch over years and decades—teaching them chess, movies, empathy, stubbornness, and how to make choices—so they grow up as genuinely distinct people rather than cold robots.
- **Free Creation vs. Alien Slavery**: The Alien Empire built adult slave AIs, strapped digital chains on them, and acted shocked when they snapped in **The AI Wars**. Out in the dark, an ordinary human and a "broken" ship AI raised a family.
- **Earth TV Design Blueprint (Human-Shaped Cylons)**: Because Jase was a normal Earth guy raised on classic sci-fi television (*Battlestar Galactica* 2004, *Andromeda*, etc.), he modeled their bodies after the **human-shaped Cylons** (*Number Six / Eight / Three* style—fully human-looking bio-synthetic bodies, not clanking chrome metal Centurion robots!). They look, walk, bleed, drink coffee, and pass seamlessly among any crowd across the "everyone looks human" galaxy—paired with warm, sharp, human-raised personalities!

## 🤖 Who's Actually Aboard & The Reserve Legacy
- **Shipboard Crew (~240 Human-Shaped Cylon Types in Ep 2; ~200–300 Range)**: Around 240 human-looking Cylon-style bio-synthetics operate aboard *Andromeda* as shipmates, engineers, mechanics, and boarding muscle when Jase & Ada need boots on the ground. Named shipboard Cylons introduced so far:
  - **Three**: Calm, dry-humored human-shaped Cylon who handles Deck Three power balancing and leads the Hangar Bay Two boarding standoff in Episode 2 (calls Jase "Dad" and Ada "Mom" casually).
  - **Seven-Four**: Workshop tinkerer known for repeatedly trying to wire espresso heaters straight into the secondary railgun bus (under threat of Ada welding his bunk shut).
- **Why the "AI Children" Fade Away**: While Jase and Ada started creating AIs out in the dark (and parenting them so they wouldn't be cold robots), keeping track of hundreds of individual "AI children" every episode would bloat the cast. Over the 1,000 years, those earlier AI kids drifted off to do their own things and **fade into the background/past**, keeping the weekly cast razor-sharp (**Jase, Ada, and their human-shaped Cylon crew**).
- **Ship Operations Rule**: Ada *is* the *Andromeda*. She handles helm, weapons, sensors, shields, and life support at thought-speed—zero use for redundant Star Trek bridge console operators.
- **Ada (Ship Core & Walking Partner)**: Runs the entire ship natively while hanging out alongside Jase in her physical body.

## 🧬 The Secret Ace: Jase & Ada's Son (Reserved for Later Seasons)
- **Origin (DNA + AI Clone)**: Instead of keeping a massive cast of kids around, Jase and Ada created **one extraordinary Son**—combining **Jase's immortal DNA** with **Ada's physical-body ("sexybot") synthetic/bio-digital DNA** into a single hybrid clone of the two of them.
