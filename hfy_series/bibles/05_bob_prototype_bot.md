# Character Bible: Bob (The Original Humanoid Maintenance Bot)

## 🪪 Core Identity
- **Name**: **Bob**
- **Origin / Chassis**: Original `X4791Z` colony-ship **humanoid maintenance bot** (non-sentient Year-1 ship hardware upgraded over decades of hands-on tinkering)
- **Role in Lore**: **The Missing Link / First Prototype** — the messy mechanical bridge between raw colony-ship repair bots and full human-shaped **Cilons**
- **Current Status (Year 1,000+)**: Still aboard *Andromeda*, one of the oldest members of the inner family—a living reminder of where everything started

---

## 🛠️ Why Bob Exists (Closing the Tech-Leap Gap)
- **Realism Rule**: Going straight from an empty colony barge in Year 1 to 240 seamless, flesh-covered bio-synthetic **Cilons** would make smart readers call BS.
- **How It Actually Happened**: When Jase and Ada first asked *"could we build a chess partner?"* out in the dark, Jase didn't jump straight to growing cloned organs and synthetic skin. He walked down to the maintenance bay, grabbed an ordinary bipedal ship repair bot—**Bob**—and spent years/decades stripping wires, swapping sensor boards, writing messy neural scaffolding, and figuring out how to raise a genuinely independent mind inside metal hardware first.
- **What Bob Taught Them**:
  1. Every mistake Jase and Ada made trying to get synthetic balance, emotion, empathy, and free will right happened on **Bob's** workbench first.
  2. By the time they later built **Ada's humanoid body** and figured out bio-synthetic **Cilon** flesh, Bob was already an old-school, fully formed person—effectively the eccentric older brother / grumpy shop uncle of every Cilon aboard!
