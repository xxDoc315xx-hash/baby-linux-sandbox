# Character Bible: Bob (The Original Humanoid Maintenance Bot)

## 🪪 Core Identity
- **Name**: **Bob**
- **Official Ship Billet**: **Executive Officer (XO)** *(Appointed by Jase simply because **Ada insisted** on having a proper warship command structure and bridge crew, even though Jase has zero practical need for an XO when Ada can just execute anything he says instantly!)*
- **Origin / Chassis**: Original `X4791Z` colony-ship **humanoid maintenance bot** (non-sentient Year-1 ship hardware upgraded over decades of Jase's early trial-and-error tinkering)
- **Timeline Position**: **Predates EVERYTHING** — built/tinkered **before Ada got her physical ("sexbot") body**, and long before the first **human-shaped Cilons** (`Three`, `Five`, `Seven-Four`) were ever raised!
- **Sentience Level**: **Barely Sentient** (Version 0.1 — perched right on the blurry line between stubborn shop-dog / R2-unit instinct and true personhood)
- **Current Status (Year 1,000+)**: Still aboard *Andromeda* in his original dented metal humanoid frame—never wiped, never "upgraded into a skinjob," and standing watch right beside the Captain's chair as the galaxy's most hilariously literal First Officer.

---

## 🛠️ Why Bob Exists (Timeline & Realism Bridge)
- **Closing the Tech-Leap Gap**: Going straight from an empty rusty colony ship in Year 1 to 240 seamless, flesh-covered **Cilons** would make smart readers call BS.
- **Actual Progression Out in the Dark**:
  1. **Early Decades (Bob Era)**: Lonely out in the black with only a ceiling-speaker AI (`Ada`) and mute repair drones, Jase grabbed an ordinary bipedal maintenance bot (**Bob**) and spent *years* hanging out with him in the machine shop—tinkering, swapping boards, and half-jokingly teaching a clunky bot how to think. Bob came out **barely sentient** and **hopelessly hard-headed**.
  2. **~Year 60 (Ada's Chassis Era)**: With what he learned wrestling with Bob's servo/neural frame, a lonely Jase started building Ada's humanoid body ("crew morale research"), slammed on the brakes via **The Airlock Rule**, and Ada claimed the finished chassis anyway.
  3. **Centuries Later (The Cilon Era)**: Using everything learned from Bob's crude frame + Ada's bio-mechanical chassis, Jase and Ada finally cracked real bio-synthetic **Cilons** and began raising/parenting full minds.
- **Why Bob Never Got Rebuilt Into a Cilon**: Jase couldn't bring himself to erase or replace his original grease-stained shop buddy from the lonely years—and Bob is far too **hard-headed** to sit still in a cradle for an upgrade anyway.

---

## 🧠 Personality, Look & Relationships (Locked Canon)
- **Physical Appearance**: Dented, heavy-duty metal humanoid maintenance frame—scuffed industrial alloy, weld scars, exposed hydraulics, mismatched replacement shoulder plates, and heavy wrench-grip hands. Zero synthetic skin. Looks like a battle-scarred shop mule standing next to glossy humans.
- **Core Trait — Hard-Headed & Barely Sentient**:
  - **Simple, Literal Mind**: Bob doesn't give philosophical speeches about freedom or read poetry. He grasps concrete things: *his pipes, his wrenches, Jase, Ada, and things that are broken.*
  - **Immovable Stubbornness**: Once Bob locks onto a job or an opinion (*"Valve leaks. Staying here."* / *"No touch."*), nobody—not Three, not Maverick, not even Ada—can easily talk him off it. If someone tries to shove him aside, he weighs six hundred pounds of welded iron and simply *does not move*.
- **Relationship With Jase**: Spent huge amounts of time alone together in the early decades before anyone else walked the halls. Bob is loyal to Jase like an old, cranky hound—follows Jase's boots on heavy repair walks, hands him the wrong wrench on purpose if Jase complains, and takes zero guff from anyone threatening the Captain.
- **Relationship With Ada**: Predates Ada's humanoid body—meaning **Bob knew Ada when she was just a voice in the ceiling!** Even 940 years later, Bob treats Ada's sleek humanoid form with mild, hard-headed side-eye (*you used to be a bulkhead speaker, stop ordering me out of Bay Four*), though he instinctively obeys *Ship Emergency* sirens.
- **Relationship With the Cilons**: The cranky barely-verbal "Great-Uncle" of the crew. **Seven-Four** is constantly trying to borrow Bob's spare copper relays; Bob chases him down hallways holding a four-foot breaker bar.

## 🔒 How Bob Acts Every Time (Episode Consistency Lock)
- **Voice & Cadence**: Minimalist, clipped, mechanical-blunt phrases (2–6 words max, or loud hydraulic grunts / heavy optical clicks when annoyed—never chatty, never eloquent).
- **Default 3-Second Stress Reaction**: **Zero tactical hesitation—pure blunt force.** Doesn't duck behind crates or calculate firing arcs; grabs the nearest fifty-pound steel pipe/welder, plants his magnetic boots, and steps straight into the danger to hit whatever broke or hurt Jase.
- **What Bob NEVER Does**: Never gives long speeches, never backs down from an argument (even when he's 100% wrong), never abandons a half-finished repair, and never willingly wears human skin or fancy uniforms.
- **Blind Spot / Flaw**: Because he's **barely sentient and hard-headed**, nuanced diplomacy and stealth completely fly over his metal head—if Jase wants a quiet sneaky negotiation, *do not let Bob wait near an unlocked tool locker!*
