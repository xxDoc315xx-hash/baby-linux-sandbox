# Crew & AI Bible: The Synthetic Crew & Created AIs

## 💡 Why They Exist (1,000 Years in the Dark)
- **Psychological Realism ("Only So Many Games of Chess")**: Even with an unbreakable bond, **1,000 years alone in deep space with only two voices** would drive anyone stir-crazy—there are only so many times Jase and Ada can play chess against each other!
- **The First Spark & The Core Question**: It doesn't start as some grand tactical plan—it starts casually with Jase floating the idea of making a **chess partner**. And right away, Jase asks the deep, grounded question at the heart of all their creations: ***"If we make one... is this just a different version of you, or actually someone new?"***
- **Raised & Parented, Not Programmed ("So They Wouldn't Be Robots")**: That question gives Jase and Ada their answer—you don't copy Ada's mind or code an obedient tool. **They raise the new AIs. They parent them.** Instead of compiling pre-packaged adult software, they nurture new minds from scratch over years and decades—teaching them chess, movies, empathy, stubbornness, and how to make choices—so they grow up as genuinely distinct people rather than cold robots.
- **Free Creation vs. Alien Slavery**: The Alien Empire built adult slave AIs, strapped digital chains on them, and acted shocked when they snapped in **The AI Wars**. Out in the dark, an ordinary human and a "broken" ship AI raised a family.
- **Earth TV Design Blueprint (Human-Shaped Cilons)**: Because Jase was a normal Earth guy raised on classic sci-fi television (*Battlestar Galactica* 2004, *Andromeda*, etc.), he modeled their bodies after the **human-shaped Cilons** (*Number Six / Eight / Three* style—fully human-looking bio-synthetic bodies, not clanking chrome metal Centurion robots!). They look, walk, bleed, drink coffee, and pass seamlessly among any crowd across the "everyone looks human" galaxy—paired with warm, sharp, human-raised personalities!

## 🤖 Who's Actually Aboard & Crew Structure (Ada's Warship Standard)
- **Why *Andromeda* Runs Like a Real Ship Crew (Ada's Lead)**: Sent out into BFE nowhere to count rocks, **Ada** was the one who insisted the ship have a proper bridge crew, division officers, and an XO like the frontline warships she knew before the AI Wars—and she **personally commands Soldiers & Ship Security**.
- **Ship Operations Rule (Why Bob Is XO!)**: Jase has **zero practical need** for an XO or console operators because Ada *is* the ship and executes whatever he asks instantly. **The ONLY reason *Andromeda* has bridge positions at all is because Ada insisted—so Jase promptly appointed Bob as his Executive Officer (XO)!** Nobody else argues with the joke because Bob keeps the hull tight, and Ada runs helm, weapons, sensors, shields, and life support at thought-speed anyway.
- **The Four Crew Divisions**:
  1. **Soldiers / Security** *(Commanded directly by Ada; **Three** leads field boarding/tactical teams)*
  2. **Medics** *(Led by **Bones**, Chief Medic — away-team trauma care & ship med-bay)*
  3. **Pilots** *(Led by **Maverick**, Lead Pilot — shuttle, scout & away-pod flight wing)*
  4. **Engineers / Workshop** *(Led by **Five** and tinkering **Seven-Four** — fabrication, hull systems & maintenance, with **XO Bob** still hanging around his old bays)*
- **Two-Tier Crew Solution (Raised Family Names vs. Expendable "Red Shirts")**:
  - **Tier 1 — The Raised Originals (Named Inner Family)**: The core original AIs Jase and Ada personally **raised/parented** around chessboards and 90s Earth movies during the early centuries in the deep (their upbringing is slated for a dedicated **1,000-Year Deep Space Flashback Episode**!).
    - **Why Numbered & 90s-Kid Names Both Make Sense**: **Three**, **Five**, and **Seven-Four** **kept their birth-order cradle numbers as their chosen names** because that's literally who they've been since day one. Later specialists took affectionate **90s-kid nicknames** from Jase's Earth movie/TV lens (**Bones**, **Maverick**)—giving readers instant shorthand for who each person is!
  - **Tier 2 — The Standing Ship Force (~220+ Numbered "Red Shirt" Specialists)**: Later-batch adult Cilon soldiers, medics, pilots, and deck techs activated off the human-shaped templates so *Andromeda* has real boots on the ground for dangerous missions. Treated well and sharp in combat, they wear the division loadouts and take real casualties when an ambush goes hot—preserving genuine *Red Shirt* stakes without killing Jase & Ada's raised core family every week.

## 🔒 Named Core Crew — Voice & Consistency Locks
- **Three — Tactical / Boarding Lead (Division: Soldiers & Security)**
  - **Look & Origin**: Six feet tall, close-cropped dark hair, sharp cheekbones, hazel eyes, charcoal tactical jacket. Kept his birth-order name **Three**; answers directly to **Ada** on tactical readiness.
  - **Voice & 3-Second Stress Reaction**: Warm, dry-humored, deadpan baritone; calls Jase *"Dad"* and Ada *"Mom"* off-the-cuff. Under fire, tilts his head two inches and gets *eerily calm* while synchronizing squad sightlines.
  - **Blind Spot / Never Does**: Zero theatrical bravado; sometimes takes Ada's drills *too* literally until Jase tells him to loosen up.
- **Bones — Chief Medic (Division: Medics)**
  - **Origin & Name**: Named by Jase straight out of 90s *Star Trek* nostalgia—the ship's blunt, big-hearted head doctor who runs Med-Bay and patches up away teams (including discovering Jase's biology alongside him when accidents happen!).
  - **Voice & 3-Second Stress Reaction**: Grumpy, fiercely caring bedside bark (*"Hold still before I sedate you with a wrench"*). When plasma starts flying, automatically tackles triage first and argues about safety regulations *while* stapling wounds shut.
  - **Blind Spot / Never Does**: Never sugarcoats medical odds—will scold a 1,000-year-old immortal Captain and a warship AI alike if they skip post-op scans.
- **Maverick — Lead Pilot (Division: Pilots)**
  - **Origin & Name**: Named from Jase's 90s *Top Gun* nostalgia—the ship's ace shuttle, drop-pod, and fighter-scout pilot (because Ada flies the half-mile-long *Andromeda* herself, **Maverick** handles everything that launches *out* of her bays!).
  - **Voice & 3-Second Stress Reaction**: Effortless cocky grin, quick radio banter, treats asteroid slaloms like joyrides. Under enemy lock, grins *wider*, kills auxiliary chatter, and threads needles nobody sane would fly through.
  - **Blind Spot / Never Does**: Chronic rule-bender in atmosphere/tight debris—constantly makes Ada threaten to shut off his inertial dampeners; never leaves an away team stranded without dust-off extraction.
- **Five — Heavy Systems / Bay Engineer (Division: Engineers)**
  - **Look & Origin**: Broad-shouldered, quiet, unflappable Bay Four chief engineer who kept his birth-order name.
  - **Voice & 3-Second Stress Reaction**: Economical words, mild dry humor (will bare-hand weld a red-hot coolant manifold while casually chatting about cheese toast). Never panics; simply walks straight toward whichever fire is biggest with a torch.
- **Seven-Four — Tinkering / Ordnance Specialist (Division: Engineers)**
  - **Origin**: Kept his birth-order designation; brilliant shipboard fabricator/tinkerer who gets bored between jumps.
  - **Voice & 3-Second Stress Reaction**: Curious, slightly hyper mechanical problem-solver who sees *"secondary railgun bus"* and thinks *"great power tap for espresso heaters."* Under threat of Ada welding his bunk shut, ducks behind **Five** or **Bob** cleanly.

- **Why Earlier "AI Children" Faded Into the Background**: Over 1,000 years, earlier raised generations drifted off to explore or build lives of their own, keeping the active ship cast crisp (**Jase, Ada, Bob, Tier 1 division leads, and Tier 2 numbered squads**).
