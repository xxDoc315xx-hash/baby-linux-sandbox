# Episode 2 Outline — `[Title TBD]` (Series Premiere, Part 2: The 1,000-Year Return)

## 🎯 Episode Goal
Deliver the **1,000-year time jump** payoff! Show how Jase, Ada, and the *Andromeda* evolved out in the dark—revealing **Ada's physical body** (via the Airlock Rule laugh), the **~200–300 Cylon crew** (raised, not programmed), and a millennium of upgrades—right as they jump back into known space and discover **the Alien Empire has fallen into ruin**, leaving **fresh, unknown space**.

---

## 📋 Scene-by-Scene Beat Sheet (YouTube Narration Pacing)

### 1. Cold Open (~60–90 sec) — 1,000 Years Later
- **Time Jump Hook**: Earth is a thousand years gone—ancient history falling away in the rearview mirror.
- Open on the *Andromeda* jumping back across the border after a millennium in the black. She looks nothing like the mothballed barge `X4791Z` that left—she's sleek, scarred, battle-hardened, and humming with 1,000 years of deep-space upgrades.
- Brief 60-second standalone anchor for any new listener clicking Ep 2 directly: who the immortal Earth-born "Prince" is, what the ship is, and why they've been gone for 1,000 years.

### 2. The Glow-Up Reveal (Ada's Body & The Cylon Crew)
- **The Bridge**: No redundant Star Trek console-pushers—Ada *is* the ship, steering and scanning at thought-speed while **walking the deck in her physical body** (*Rommie*-style) right alongside Jase.
- **Why She Has the Body (The Airlock Rule Laugh)**: Quick, hilarious nod to how Ada ended up with an insanely attractive android chassis—decades into the dark, a lonely Jase started building a "companion body," stopped dead in his tracks realizing *"never cross that line with the person who controls my oxygen and airlocks,"* and Ada claimed the body anyway so they could explore planets together in person while staying **100% strictly platonic**.
- **The Cylon Crew (~200–300 Strong)**: Gleaming *Battlestar*-inspired Cylon synthetics walking the colony decks—built off the sci-fi TV Jase grew up watching, and **parented/raised** out in the dark rather than chained like Empire slave AIs (with earlier generations having drifted off to do their own things, keeping the active ship crew tight and sharp).
- *(Reserve Rule: Do **NOT** reveal Jase & Ada's DNA-AI hybrid **Son** yet—save him strictly off-screen for a later season when needed!)*

### 3. The Big Reveal: The Empire Fell Into Ruin
- Jase half-expects an arrogant Peacekeeper patrol to hail them and demand why `X4791Z` took 1,000 years to count rocks.
- Instead, Ada's sensors sweep across empty beacons, shattered orbital stations, and unfamiliar territory signatures.
- **The Shock**: **The Alien Empire that made Jase a Prince has collapsed into ruin.** Whatever wiped them out or broke them apart happened centuries ago.
- **Fresh Unknown Space**: No imperial borders. No Peacekeeper fleet. Just fractured ruins, strange new powers, and completely **fresh, unknown space**.

### 5. Clean Button & Series Launch (Setting Up Episode 3+)
- Standing over the star map of **fresh, unknown space**, Jase realizes his bogus 1,000-year survey mission finally has real meaning—nobody left alive even remembers the chain of command that exiled him.
- A whole uncharted galaxy of imperial ruins, new species, and adventures stretches out ahead—launching the standalone weekly TV-show format starting in **Episode 3**.
