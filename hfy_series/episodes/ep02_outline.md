# Episode 2 Outline — *Ghosts of an Empire* (Series Premiere, Part 2: The 1,000-Year Return)

## 🎯 Episode Goal
Deliver the **1,000-year time jump** payoff! Show how Jase, Ada, and the *Andromeda* evolved out in the dark—revealing **Ada's physical body** (via the Airlock Rule laugh), the **~240 Cilon crew** (raised, not programmed), and a millennium of upgrades—right as they jump back into known space and discover **the Alien Empire collapsed into ruin (~600 years ago in The Great Fracture)**, leaving **fresh, unknown space**.

---

## 📋 Scene-by-Scene Beat Sheet (YouTube Narration Pacing)

### 1. Cold Open (~60–90 sec) — 1,000 Years Later
- **Time Jump Hook**: Earth is a thousand years gone—ancient history falling away in the rearview mirror.
- Open on the *Andromeda* jumping back across the border after a millennium in the black. She looks nothing like the mothballed barge `X4791Z` that left—she's sleek, scarred, battle-hardened, and humming with 1,000 years of deep-space upgrades.
- Brief 60-second standalone anchor for any new listener clicking Ep 2 directly: who the immortal Earth-born "Prince" is, what the ship is, and why they've been gone for 1,000 years.

### 2. The Glow-Up Reveal (Ada's Body & The Cilon Crew)
- **The Bridge**: No redundant Star Trek console-pushers—Ada *is* the ship, steering and scanning at thought-speed while **walking the deck in her physical body** (*Rommie*-style) right alongside Jase.
- **Why She Has the Body (The Airlock Rule Laugh)**: Quick, hilarious nod to how Ada ended up with an insanely attractive android chassis—decades into the dark, a lonely Jase started building a "companion body," stopped dead in his tracks realizing *"never cross that line with the person who controls my oxygen and airlocks,"* and Ada claimed the body anyway so they could explore planets together in person while staying **100% strictly platonic**.
- **The Cilon Crew (~240 Strong)**: **Human-shaped** *Battlestar*-inspired bio-synthetic Cilons walking the colony decks in crisp shipboard jackets (including **Three** and workshop tinkerer **Seven-Four**)—built off the sci-fi TV Jase grew up watching, and **parented/raised** out in the dark rather than chained like Empire slave AIs (with earlier generations having drifted off to do their own things, keeping the active ship crew tight and sharp).
- *(Reserve Rule: Do **NOT** reveal Jase & Ada's DNA-AI hybrid **Son** yet—save him strictly off-screen for a later season when needed!)*

### 3. The Big Reveal: The Empire Fell Into Ruin
- Jase half-expects an arrogant Peacekeeper patrol to hail them and demand why `X4791Z` took 1,000 years to count rocks.
- Instead, Ada's sensors sweep across Beacon Nine (cut cleanly in half), empty relays, and dead military frequencies (`SIGNAL LOSS — CENTURY UNKNOWN`).
- **The Shock**: **The Alien Empire that made Jase a Prince has collapsed into ruin.**
- **Fresh Unknown Space**: No imperial borders. No Peacekeeper fleet. Just fractured ruins, patchwork unencrypted radio ripples, and completely **fresh, unknown space**.

### 4. Episode A-Plot: First Contact in the New Frontier (Locked Canon)
- **The Distress Call**: Six hours inward near a scrap-heavy planetary belt, *Andromeda* intercepts a distress burst from Captain **Maren**, flying a battered civilian freighter carrying medicine and seed stock for forty families on **Haven Three**.
- **The Bullies**: Three mismatched salvaged gunboats from the **Red Ledger Syndicate** have her pinned down, demanding she vent her water and surrender.
- **The Trap & Hangar Bay Two Standoff**: Jase parks half a mile of silent colony hull (`X4791Z`) between Maren and the raiders. Thinking she's a drifting antique museum piece, **Sergeant Kell** and ~22 Syndicate raiders breach into **Hangar Bay Two**—only to walk into bright arc lights and 24 razor-sharp **human-shaped Cilon crew** led by **Three**, while **Ada** seals the boarding hatch and opens 20 concealed broadside weapon blisters & sapphire shields outside against their gunboats.
- **The Lore Drop & Reward**: After sending the humbled Syndicate gunboats limping home, Jase and Ada host Captain Maren in the upper galley over real Earth-heirloom coffee. Maren explains **The Great Fracture** (~600 years ago: rival warlord houses, succession wars over missing bloodlines, and banned AI vaults) and gifts Jase a copper data-chip mapping safe jump lanes, trade relays, and dead zones within 200 light-years.

### 5. Clean Button & Series Launch (Setting Up Episode 3+)
- Slotting Maren's data-chip into the holo-table lights up frontier waypoints ending at `UNMAPPED BEYOND THIS POINT`.
- Standing over the star map of **fresh, unknown space**, Jase laughs off Ada's joke about telling the galaxy he's legally a sovereign Prince of an empire that died 600 years ago—and sets course past **Haven Three** into the uncharted dark, launching the standalone weekly TV-show format in **Episode 3**.
