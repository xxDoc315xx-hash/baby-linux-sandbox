# Episode 1 Outline — `[Title TBD]` (Series Premiere, Part 1: Origin & Year 1)

## 🎯 Episode Goal
Establish **Jase Hill**, the Peacekeeper-style **Alien Empire**, **The Chair**, the **Immortal Protected Race** loophole, and **Ada** aboard mothballed colony ship `X4791Z` (renamed ***Andromeda***). Deliver a funny, emotionally grounded standalone story about two Empire rejects trapped together in Year 1—ending as they vanish into their 1,000-year exile.

---

## 📋 Scene-by-Scene Beat Sheet (YouTube Narration Pacing)

### 1. Cold Open (~60–90 sec)
- Hook: Earth traded **100,000 humans** to an alien mercenary empire for the promise of advanced technology. Most people thought it was a suicide ticket. **Jase Hill**—early 40s, nothing left tying him to Earth—volunteered anyway.
- Establish the dry, grounded Earth-guy tone immediately: Jase isn't a chosen one or a superhero—he's just tired of spinning his wheels and figures space can't be worse than Tuesday.

### 2. The Chair & The Conversion
- The Peacekeeper-style aliens (`[Species/Empire Name TBD]`) put recruits into **"The Chair"** (*Farscape* Aurora Chair style) to test worthiness and reveal what role each person would be good for.
- Some flunk out immediately as unfit; others don't survive the brutal conversion process when the aliens "work on everyone."
- **The Twist**: Jase survives—and comes out an **immortal human version** of them, keeping his human mind, dry humor, and stubborn free will.

### 3. The Protected Race Loophole ("Prince in Name Only")
- **Galaxy Law Problem**: **Immortals are a legally protected race**. The aliens can't shoot Jase, dissect him, or throw him into disposable infantry meat-grinders without violating protected-race statutes.
- Stuck honoring his status while hating every second of it, they slap him with an empty title (**Prince in name only**) and decide to bury the problem quietly.
- Their solution: hand him a forgotten, mothballed colony ship (`X4791Z`), pair him with a "broken" ship AI who refused orders during **The AI Wars**, and send them on a bogus **1,000-year survey mission** off the edge of the map.

### 4. Meeting *Andromeda* & Ada (Year 1)
- Jase boards `X4791Z`—a massive, self-sustaining modified colony ship built so someone could live comfortably forever without dealing with anyone else.
- He refuses to call his home a license plate number and names her ***Andromeda*** (after the TV show he remembers from Earth).
- **Important Year-1 Rule**: Jase knows **nothing** about AIs, the AI Wars, or Ada's former warship past—and Ada doesn't bring it up. To him, she's just a sharp-tongued ship voice running his rusty barge and mute maintenance robots.

### 5. A-Plot Conflict: "Ship vs. Jase" (The Airlock Standoff)
- Early into the voyage, Jase gets restless, grabs a pack, and heads for the hatch to step out onto a station/rock (`[Exact errand/location TBD]`)—"I'm going out to get supplies."
- *CLUNK.* Magnetic deadbolts slam shut.
  **Ada:** *"No, you aren't."*
- No tactical jargon or warship speeches—just pure blunt honesty: **"If you leave and get stuck out there, I'd be all alone with the robots."**
- **Resolution / Bonding**: Realizing they're both unwanted castoffs dumped into deep space to be forgotten, they call a truce. Over endless games of chess in the dark—realizing *"there's only so many times we can play chess against each other"*—Jase floats the first wild idea of building a third player... and Ada asks if they're really going to spend 1,000 years playing by the Empire's rules.

### 6. Clean Button / Transition to Part 2
- Jase leans back, looks at the empty deep-space star charts, and makes a quiet call: *"Screw their survey. We've got a colony ship, a thousand years, and nobody watching."*
- *Cue the 1,000-year horizon—leading straight into Part 2 (Episode 2).*
