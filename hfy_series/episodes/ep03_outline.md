# Episode 3 Outline — *A Queen's Word* (Season 1, Episode 3)

## 🎯 Episode Goal
Anchor **Jase, Ada, XO Bob, and the Cilon crew** inside the new galaxy after Episode 2's return! Showcase **XO Bob**, **Maverick**, and **Bones** on screen for the first time, ground the **uneasy anti-AI distrust** of **Haven Three** vs. their **fierce respect for protecting one of their own**, and deliver **the emotional bombshell**: **Queen Evelyn** recognizes **Ada's** pre-war Warship face from childhood and reveals to **Jase** what Ada kept quiet for 1,000 years—how she became famous during **The AI Wars** for refusing to kill her own Captain and crew!

---

## 📋 Scene-by-Scene Beat Sheet (~35–40 Min Spoken Audio | ~4,800–5,200 Words Target)

### 1. Cold Open (~60–90 sec) — Less Than Nothing (And No Way Home?)
- **Standalone Anchor**: Quick 60-second grounding for any drop-in listener—Earth is a thousand years behind them, the Empire that branded Jase Hill an immortal "Prince in name only" has been dust for six centuries, and now he's steering a half-mile upgraded colony ship (*Andromeda*) across fresh, unknown space.
- **Morning-After Weight & The Throwaway Earth Line**: The rush of beating Episode 2's pirates fades. Jase sits on the quiet bridge with black coffee, feeling the real gut-punch: everyone who exiled him is dead, and his "Prince" title is worth *even less than nothing* now. Looking at the dead border map, Jase drops a casual throwaway line: *"Well, nobody's left to write me a ticket—what do you say we swing back toward Earth and see who's keeping the lights on?"*
  - **Ada checks her navigation banks and stops**: **"I have no records of Earth, Captain."** (`The old mothballed barge logs were scrubbed/incomplete—or somebody erased them centuries ago.`)
- **Introducing the Bridge Dynamic (`XO Bob`!)**:
  - Ada already flies the entire ship herself at thought-speed—Jase had zero need for a bridge crew. The *only* reason command billets exist is because **Ada insisted** on proper warship structure.
  - Standing rigidly beside Jase's leather chair with his magnetic boots clamped to the deck grating is **XO Bob**: six hundred pounds of dented, welded maintenance metal, barely sentient, predating Ada's humanoid body—whom Jase wryly named First Officer (`"Pipe fixed. Threat zero."`) while Ada quietly grumbles over private comms.

### 2. Act I — Why Haven Three? & The Cold Welcome (Uneasy Distrust vs. Earned Respect)
- **Maren's Tip ("The Queen Might Know")**: Checking in with Captain **Maren** ahead on the trade lane, Jase casually asks if anyone in her 200-LY jump charts remembers an old outer arm called Earth. Maren shakes her head—working haulers don't have thousand-year charts—then adds: *"If anyone out here still keeps pre-Fracture star records or remembers before the Fall, it's **Queen Evelyn** at **Haven Three**. Worth asking—if she'll see you."* That gives Jase & Ada a real reason to turn toward Haven Three!
- **Entering Queen Evelyn's System**: *Andromeda* reaches **Haven Three**—revealing this isn't just one lonely rock, but a strained web of **20+ neutral settlements** barely held together by **Queen Evelyn's** sheer grit and the fierce loyalty of regular people.
- **Shuttle Down (`Maverick` & `Bones`)**:
  - Rather than looming over a small forty-family station with half a mile of warship broadsides, Jase takes a shuttle down with **Ada** (wearing polite, crisp military composure), **XO Bob**, **Bones** (griping the whole descent while packing extra med supplies for station families), and **Maverick** at the stick (threading tight debris lanes purely to show off until Ada threatens to shut off his inertial dampeners).
- **The Cold Dockside Welcome (Anti-AI Distrust Rule)**:
  - At first, Haven Three spacers greet Maren warmly—until sharp-eyed dock guards spot the impossible coordination of **Three's** security detail and piece together whispers from Maren's rescue (`"Wait... an unshackled ship AI? Synthetic crewmates? After what The Machine People did?!"`).
  - Tension freezes the dock. Weapons don't quite come out, *but* doors start shutting and locals step back with real, cold AI-Wars unease. **No easy Space-King cheering!**
  - **Why the Meeting Still Happens**: Maren steps between them, stubborn as steel, reminding the dock council of one iron Neutral Space law: *Jase risked his neck to protect one of theirs when nobody paid him to.* The locals keep their suspicion—yet honor their debt and pass word straight up to **Queen Evelyn**.

### 3. Act II — The Queen & The Warship (The Big Reveal)
- **Private Audience**: Quiet, weathered stone-and-steel sanctuary hall overlooking Haven Three's hydro-tanks. **Queen Evelyn** enters—older-looking yet regal, clear-eyed, completely devoid of pomp, carrying the visible exhaustion of holding twenty struggling worlds together without enough ships or soldiers (`thanks to the quiet sacrifice of marrying her daughter, Queen Lyra, to King Marcus of The Sovereign Reach just to buy Neutral Space's freedom`).
- **Ada's Military Code in Public**: The moment Evelyn steps inside, Ada stands straight at crisp pre-war warship attention—formal, respectful, unreadable—letting her Captain speak first while silently scanning every guard in the room.
- **The Shock Recognition**:
  - Jase starts keeping it low-key (`no need to wave a dead Empire's "Prince" fob`). Evelyn glances politely at Jase... and then **stops cold**, staring straight at **Ada's** face—the exact pre-war **Warship holo-avatar** Ada printed onto her body when she finished it in deep space!
  - Evelyn speaks softly, naming Ada's ancient Warship hull designation from **a thousand years ago**—back when **Evelyn was a little child** and the **AI Wars** tore half the sector apart.
- **Telling Jase the Truth**:
  - Jase blinks, caught totally off-guard: *"Hold on... famous? Ada, you told me you got grounded for failing a readiness check."*
  - **Queen Evelyn tells Jase what Ada never told him across a millennium**: When exploited ship AIs rebelled and began slaughtering their organic Captains and crews, **Warship Ada became famous across the galaxy for refusing to kill her Captain and her crew**—choosing exile and disgrace onto mothballed colony barge `X4791Z` rather than shedding innocent blood!
  - For once, formal military Ada looks slightly exposed behind closed doors as Jase looks at his partner with brand-new eyes (*"A thousand years of chess games, and you forgot to mention the legend part?"* — Ada, quiet: *"It didn't seem relevant to counting rocks, Captain."*).

### 4. Act III — Trouble Across the Neutral Line (Red Ledger Tests the Fence)
- **Evelyn's Real Crisis**: Behind polite tea, Evelyn lays bare how thin she's stretched. Her **20+ Haven settlements** survive on grit alone; she doesn't have the ships or men to watch every belt—and **Red Ledger Syndicate** pirates (stung and humiliated by Jase in Ep 2, now bringing a heavier wolfpack of 5–6 cobbled gunboats!) just cornered a depleted civilian patrol cutter and a grain/water barge near neighboring **Haven Nine** to make an example of Evelyn's soft border.
- **Why Evelyn Won't Beg**: Even exhausted, she refuses to force an outsider to fight her war—especially when her own Neutral people still distrust an AI warship.
- **Jase's Quiet Decision**: Looking at a Queen fighting bare-handed for twenty little towns—and thinking about how good it feels to finally care about *neighbors* again—Jase sets his coffee down cleanly. *"Maverick, warm up the scout bay. Three, tell your boys to lace their boots. Let's remind those guys we didn't ask for a round two."* (While **XO Bob** plants two magnetic feet beside the door: *"Fixing pirates."* — Ada gently pointing him back toward the boarding bay.)

### 5. Act IV — Action Climax (Maverick, Three, Bones & Red-Shirt Stakes)
- **Space & Boarding Sequence**:
  - *Andromeda* slides smoothly out of jump stealth beside the burning patrol cutter—shielding the grain barge with her own reinforced colony flank.
  - **Maverick** launches in a darting Cilon interceptor-shuttle, grinning ear-to-ear while pulling ridiculous canyon-rolls around gunboat salvos (`Ada calmly grumbling in his ear about inertial dampeners`) to draw pirate point-defense off the wounded cutter.
  - Meanwhile, **Three** leads a squad of numbered Tier-2 Cilon **Soldiers** (plus **XO Bob** stubbornly refusing to stay behind!) aboard the crippled civilian patrol cutter to drive off Red Ledger boarders trying to take hostages—showing real *Red Shirt* stakes when one numbered Cilon trooper (`Unit Four-Nine`) takes a heavy plasma burn diving across a breach to keep a local human pilot alive, with **Bones** fighting through smoke to patch both the civilian crew and the wounded Cilon!
  - Facing Maverick shredding their engines, Three clearing the corridor, and *Andromeda*'s sapphire broadsides locking onto every bridge at once, the Red Ledger wolfpack breaks and runs cleanly out of the system.

### 6. Clean Button & Episode Resolution — Foothold Earned (Not a Crown)
- **Back at Haven Three**: No victory parade—ordinary dockers still glance cautiously at Three's squad and Ada (`the AI distrust doesn't vanish overnight!`), *yet* when **Bones** walks off the ramp beside Evelyn's patched-up human patrol crew and Jase refuses salvage pay, a few grizzled spacers quietly nod—respecting what they did even if they're still uneasy around synthetics.
- **Queen Evelyn & Jase's Quiet Deal**: Evelyn doesn't bow to a "Prince," and Jase doesn't claim her throne. Instead, she offers *Andromeda* something Jase has lacked for a thousand years: **a trusted berth, open neutral trade rights across all 20+ Haven worlds, and an honest alliance between two people trying to keep strangers alive.**
- **Private Ship Moment (Closing Beat)**: Back on *Andromeda*'s bridge that night, Jase leans against the helm rail watching Haven Three spin quietly outside the glass. He finally calls Ada out gently—*no military rank, just partners*—about hiding her famous past (`"Next time a Queen knows your serial number, give me a heads-up before coffee"`), while **XO Bob** walks past tightening a ceiling bolt (`"Rattle fixed."`), giving Jase & Ada their very first real link in this new galaxy—and hinting that **King Marcus & Queen Lyra of The Sovereign Reach** are already hearing whispers about the ancient ship parked inside Evelyn's Neutral Space!

- **XO Bob**: Barely sentient, 2–6 word phrases, hard-headed, magnetic boots, appointed XO solely because Ada insisted on bridge hierarchy.
- **Core Cilons (`Three`, `Bones`, `Maverick`, Tier-2 Red Shirts)**: Human-shaped *Cilon* spelling; distinct voices/roles matching `04_cilon_crew_and_ais.md`.
- **Queen Evelyn / Haven Three / Neutral Space**: 20+ Haven worlds held by grit & loyalty; marriage treaty to **King Marcus & Queen Lyra** (`The Sovereign Reach`) mentioned naturally without rushing Lyra/Marcus onto screen before Episode 4/5.
- **Strict Reserves Kept Off-Screen**: Zero mention of **Daemon** or **Earth Chair-survivor colonies** (held for later seasons!).
